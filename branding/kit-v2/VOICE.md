# Voice and sharing

Main headline: **Your data. Your frontier.**

Alternate headlines:

- A little swagger. A lot of Postgres.
- Postgres, with room to explore.

Short product description: DB Cowboy is a native Windows Postgres workspace for SQL, tables, relationships and embedding exploration.

Long description: DB Cowboy brings independent SQL tabs, staged row editing, charts, visual query plans, relationships and pgvector exploration into a self-contained WinUI app. Connect to an existing local server, Docker port or remote database. Rowdy, the living database cowboy, gives the workspace its character.

Write controls literally: “Run statement,” “Review & apply,” “Find a table.” Keep cowboy personality for welcome and expressive moments: “Meet your new Postgres sidekick.” Never hide errors behind jokes or turn destructive operations into catchphrases.

Use the icon/profile art with one eye-width of breathing room. Keep Rowdy's hat, face and boots in social crops. The banner places the character on the right and copy on the left; do not crop away the face to preserve a decorative edge. Use the dark hero on night surfaces and the original on light surfaces. Keep Dash and Dot visually supporting the main mascot.

No company history, personal biography, social handle, contact address, testimonial or legal policy is invented. Add destination-specific metadata only when there is a real public destination.
