# DB Cowboy — Rowdy

DB Cowboy is a native Windows Postgres workspace for experienced developers. Its identity should make a capable everyday instrument feel personal, memorable and lightly mischievous. The main mascot is **Rowdy**, a living database wearing a swept copper cowboy hat. Dash, an angular index character, and Dot, a rounded embedding sprite, support him. There are **three avatar identities and four reactions**.

## The idea

The cowboy is literally the database: a warm cream cylinder face sits over two petrol-teal data tiers. An oversized asymmetric hat, lifted brow and crooked smile supply personality without relying on a caption. The broad hat and stable stacked body create deliberate tension. Three alternatives were critiqued before selection: living data cowboy, desert wind creature and mechanical jackrabbit. The latter two were weaker in product specificity or small-size face ownership.

Rowdy owns the broad copper hat silhouette. Dash has an amber diamond face and shorter teal cap; Dot has three broad connected mint lobes around a cream round face. They share ink contours and broad cel planes, rather than identical anatomy or interchangeable costumes. All four reactions use Rowdy so that expression strengthens the lead mascot rather than introducing an unrelated cast.

## Masters and use

The canonical logo is authored vector geometry plus licensed Manrope outlines. The full-body mascot is original ImageGen illustration developed from the exact icon reference. These are distinct asset roles with explicit ancestry; the illustration is not represented as an SVG export.

- `logo/package/svg/`: approved primary, secondary, symbol, wordmark, monochrome and reversed masters.
- `icons/windows.ico`: transparent Windows icon, 16/24/32/48/64/128/256 embedded sizes. `app-1024.png` is the high-resolution source.
- `graphics/rowdy-hero.png` and `rowdy-hero-dark.png`: matching transparent hero art. Dark art is a separate reference-controlled generation with lifted slate edges and warm boot/hat highlights.
- `packs/avatars/`: the three avatar PNGs. No light/dark duplicates.
- `src/DbCowboy/Assets/reactions/` (in the app, not in this kit): the four Rowdy reactions. Each has a light and a dark PNG and one transparent GIF.

Keep at least one eye width of clear air around the mascot and one lowercase o around lockups. Use the full horizontal logo at 180px or wider and the expressive symbol at 24px or wider. At 16px the symbol is an intentionally reduced hat/cylinder silhouette; do not expect the full facial expression. The monochrome mark intentionally reduces to hat and cylinder masses. Ink wordmarks belong on light surfaces, reversed cream wordmarks on dark. Never stretch, retype, add a badge, trace a delivery PNG or use the full-body drawing as a tiny taskbar icon.

## Color and type

The integration authority is `brand-contract.json`. Copper #C76335 supplies character, cream #FFF0D2 the face, teal #287F80 the data body, ink #19272E the contour, amber #F5B761 highlights, paper #F8F4EC light canvas and night #101B21 dark canvas. Text/action copper is the darker #A64F22 so normal text and white-on-accent controls retain contrast. Decoration does not replace semantic error/success cues.

Manrope weight 800 forms the logo, shaped by HarfBuzz and outlined with fontTools. The unchanged supporting variable font and SIL OFL 1.1 license ship in the kit. Native dense controls retain Windows system typography; Segoe UI is not redistributed. SQL uses the existing system editor face. Brand headings are heavy and tightly spaced; body text remains calm and spacious.

## Native app and distribution

The compact header, executable resource, title-bar icon and Inno Setup icon use the same Rowdy master. A native Home page presents the lead mascot above two real actions, followed by the three supporting connection avatars. Hero height responds to window height; content scrolls when required. The working SQL/data surfaces keep their existing information density and native controls. Home navigation returns to the mascot without introducing decorative motion into query work.

Connection identities are optional. Existing unknown legacy avatar names safely fall back to Plain in the editor; connection credentials and queries remain independent of the brand. All artwork is shipped locally in the self-contained portable and installer payloads. No web runtime is introduced into the WinUI app.

The installer has a deliberate welcome/finish sequence, approved logo lockup, full dark-surface Rowdy art and native destination controls. Windows ICO, setup resource and installer artwork are verified independently from source configuration. Builds remain unsigned.

## Voice

The voice is direct and capable. Personality belongs in the mascot and welcome copy; SQL controls continue to say what they do. Main tagline: **Your data. Your frontier.** Short description: **A native Windows Postgres workspace with SQL, data editing and embedding exploration.** No fabricated company history, testimonials, customers or contacts are included.
